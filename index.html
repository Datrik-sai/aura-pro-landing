Stage I — The Form

Designed to disappear.

Every curve is shaped for hours of comfort you stop noticing. A titanium frame and hand-stitched protein leather, balanced to rest on you rather than press.

Stage II — The Craft

Material obsession.

Brushed titanium hinges. Memory-foam cushions wrapped in grained protein leather. A hairline of champagne gold where metal meets driver. Every surface considered.

Stage III — The Technology

Silence on demand.

The technology lives inside the form, not on top of it. A 50mm planar driver sits at the centre of each ear cup, while microphones in the housing listen to the room so the noise cancellation can adapt as it changes.

Why AURA Pro

Engineered for the uncompromising.

Four decisions define how AURA Pro sounds, feels and lasts. None of them were the easy option.

  1. 01

    Spatial Audio

    Music placed around you, not inside your head. Spatial audio processing gives every instrument its own place in a three-dimensional soundstage, rendered through the 50mm planar driver in each ear cup.

    360°Soundstage
  2. 02

    Adaptive ANC

    Trains, offices, open-plan cafés — the cancellation re-tunes itself as your surroundings change, so you never reach for a setting. The world fades. Your music stays.

    200×Adjustments a second*
  3. 03

    60-Hour Battery

    A long-haul flight and the week after it, on one charge. Running low? Ten minutes on the cable returns five more hours. Charge less, listen more.

    10minFor five more hours
  4. 04

    Titanium Frame

    Light enough to forget on long sessions, strong enough to live in a bag. Grade 5 titanium is lighter than steel and far stronger — built to outlast everything else you carry.

    Gr. 5Titanium

* Figures on this page are concept design targets for a fictional product, not measured results.
